# ✈️ Airline Operations & Revenue Analysis

SQL-based analysis of airline database data to explore aircraft capacity, ticket bookings, revenue, fares, occupancy, and operational performance.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes an airline database using **SQL and Python** to extract business insights related to airline operations and revenue performance.

The analysis focuses on aircraft capacity, ticket bookings, revenue trends, fare conditions, aircraft-level performance, and occupancy rates.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis produced the following business insights:

### ✈️ Aircraft Capacity

Six aircraft types have more than 100 seats:

* **773:** 402 seats
* **763:** 222 seats
* **321:** 170 seats
* **320:** 140 seats
* **733:** 130 seats
* **319:** 116 seats

The **773 aircraft has the highest seating capacity with 402 seats**.

### 🎟️ Ticket Booking Trend

Ticket bookings increased rapidly during the early part of the analyzed period, rising from **6 bookings on June 21, 2017** to more than **9,500 bookings by August 14, 2017**.

The booking volume generally stabilized at a high level after the initial growth period.

### 💰 Revenue Trend

Daily booking revenue increased substantially during the analyzed period.

Daily revenue reached approximately **524.6 million** on **August 14, 2017**, the highest value observed in the dataset.

### 💵 Average Fare by Aircraft

Business-class fares were consistently higher than Economy fares across aircraft where both fare conditions were available.

For example:

* Aircraft **319:** Business ≈ 113.6K vs Economy ≈ 38.3K
* Aircraft **763:** Business ≈ 82.8K vs Economy ≈ 27.6K
* Aircraft **773:** Business ≈ 57.8K vs Economy ≈ 19.3K

This demonstrates the significant revenue difference between premium and economy fare conditions.

### 🏆 Aircraft Revenue Performance

The highest total revenue was generated by:

1. **SU9 — 5.11B**
2. **763 — 4.37B**
3. **773 — 3.43B**
4. **319 — 2.71B**
5. **CR2 — 1.98B**

**SU9 generated the highest total revenue among the aircraft analyzed.**

### 🎫 Average Revenue per Ticket

Aircraft **319** generated the highest average revenue per ticket at approximately **51.2K**, followed by:

* 763 — **35.0K**
* 773 — **23.8K**
* 733 — **16.6K**
* 321 — **15.3K**
* SU9 — **14.0K**

This indicates that aircraft-level revenue performance is influenced not only by ticket volume but also by the average value of tickets sold.

### 🪑 Aircraft Occupancy

Average occupancy rates varied considerably across aircraft.

| Aircraft | Occupancy Rate |
| -------- | -------------: |
| 773      |     **65.90%** |
| 733      |     **61.73%** |
| SU9      |     **58.57%** |
| 321      |     **52.24%** |
| 763      |     **51.32%** |
| CN1      |     **50.04%** |
| 319      |     **46.19%** |
| CR2      |     **42.97%** |

The **773 aircraft had the highest average occupancy at 65.90%**, while **CR2 had the lowest at 42.97%**.

### 📈 10% Occupancy Improvement Scenario

A scenario analysis was performed assuming that the occupancy rate of every aircraft could be increased by **10% relative to its current rate**.

Based on the notebook's calculation:

* Current total aircraft revenue: **20.77B**
* Projected revenue: **22.84B**
* Potential increase: approximately **2.08B**
* Potential increase: approximately **10%**

This demonstrates the potential financial impact of improving aircraft seat utilization.

> **Note:** This is a scenario analysis, not a forecast. It assumes revenue scales proportionally with the increase in occupancy.

## 🎯 Business Takeaways

Based on the analysis:

* **SU9** generated the highest total revenue.
* **319** generated the highest average revenue per ticket.
* **773** had the highest seating capacity and the highest occupancy rate.
* **CR2** had the lowest occupancy rate, indicating potential room for improving seat utilization.
* Business-class fares generated substantially higher average ticket values than Economy fares.
* A hypothetical **10% relative improvement in occupancy** could increase calculated revenue by approximately **2.08B**, assuming revenue scales proportionally with occupancy.
